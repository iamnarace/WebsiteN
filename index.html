Helpful Local Guides

Useful advice before you book support

Simple checks for two common home and small-business technology problems.

Start at the router

Test close to the router, compare a wired connection where possible, then restart the NBN box and router once.

Check placement

  • Use an open, central and elevated location.
  • Avoid metal cabinets, microwaves and thick masonry.
  • Consider mesh only after confirming the NBN connection itself is healthy.

Protect identities first

  • Require multifactor authentication.
  • Use separate everyday and administrator accounts.
  • Review forwarding rules and inactive users.

Protect devices and information

Keep systems updated, use encryption and maintain an independent backup of critical information.
